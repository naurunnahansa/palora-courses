Nuclear Power Station

Advantages
No greenhouse gases during operation
Very high energy output per kg of fuel
Reliable — runs 24/7 regardless of weather
Small amount of fuel needed
Disadvantages
Radioactive waste — dangerous for thousands of years
Risk of nuclear accidents (meltdown)
Very expensive to build and decommission
Non-renewable (uranium is finite)
Nuclear power uses controlled fission to generate heat, which produces steam to drive turbines and generators.
