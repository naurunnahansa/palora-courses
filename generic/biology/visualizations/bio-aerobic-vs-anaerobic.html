Key comparison: Aerobic respiration produces ~19x more ATP per glucose molecule than anaerobic. Anaerobic is faster but much less efficient. Lactic acid must later be broken down (requiring oxygen — the "oxygen debt"). Fermentation in yeast is used in bread-making (CO2) and brewing (ethanol).
