The Transpiration Stream

Water moves from soil through roots, up the stem via xylem, and evaporates from leaves

Key Processes

Transpiration: Evaporation of water from leaf surfaces through stomata, creating a pull.

Cohesion-tension: Water molecules stick together (cohesion) and to xylem walls (adhesion), forming a continuous column pulled upward.

Osmosis: Water enters root hair cells from the soil down a water potential gradient.
