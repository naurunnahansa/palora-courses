Humid Layer Around a Leaf

Wind affects the layer of water vapour (humid layer) around a leaf, which in turn affects the rate of transpiration.

Still air
Windy
Still Air
Thick humid layer = slow transpiration
Windy Conditions
Wind removes humid layer = fast transpiration

In still air, water vapour accumulates around the leaf, forming a thick humid layer. This reduces the diffusion gradient, slowing transpiration. Wind blows this layer away, maintaining a steep diffusion gradient and increasing the rate of water loss through stomata.
