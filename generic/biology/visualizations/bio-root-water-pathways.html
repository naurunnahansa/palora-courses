Water Pathways Through the Root

Three routes water can take from root hair cells to the xylem

Pathway Descriptions

Symplast: Water moves through cytoplasm, crossing between cells via plasmodesmata.
Apoplast: Water moves through cell walls and intercellular spaces. Blocked by the Casparian strip at the endodermis.
Vacuolar: Water passes through the vacuoles of cells by osmosis, from cell to cell.
