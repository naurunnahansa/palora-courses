Key Facts

  • Complementary base pairing: A-T (2 hydrogen bonds), C-G (3 hydrogen bonds)
  • Hydrogen bonds hold the two strands together
  • Strands are antiparallel: one runs 5' to 3', the other 3' to 5'
  • Sugar-phosphate backbone provides structural support
  • The double helix has a major and minor groove
