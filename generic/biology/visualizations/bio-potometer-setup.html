Potometer Setup

A potometer measures the rate of water uptake by a plant shoot, which gives an estimate of the rate of transpiration.

Important: The potometer measures the rate of water uptake, not transpiration directly. However, most water absorbed is lost through transpiration, so it gives a good estimate. The air bubble moves towards the shoot as water is taken up. The faster the bubble moves, the greater the rate of water uptake.

The bubble animates to show the direction of water uptake as transpiration occurs.
