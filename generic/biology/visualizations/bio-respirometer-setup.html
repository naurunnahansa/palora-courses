Respirometer

Measures the rate of oxygen consumption during aerobic respiration. The animated fluid shows how the manometer responds as organisms use oxygen.

How it works: As the organisms respire, they use up O₂. The CO₂ they produce is absorbed by the soda lime. This means the total gas volume in the experimental tube decreases, lowering pressure. The colored fluid in the manometer moves towards the experimental side. The control tube (with non-living glass beads) shows no change, confirming the fluid movement is due to respiration.
Why soda lime? Without soda lime, CO₂ produced would replace the O₂ consumed, and the gas volume would not change. By removing CO₂, only the O₂ decrease is measured, allowing us to calculate the rate of oxygen consumption.
