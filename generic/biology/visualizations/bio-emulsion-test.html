Emulsion Test for Lipids

A simple test to detect the presence of fats and oils in a food sample.

1
Crush sample
Crush or chop the food sample into a test tube.
2
Add ethanol
Add ethanol and shake well to dissolve any lipids.
3
Pour into water
Pour the ethanol solution into a test tube of cold water.
4
Observe result
Toggle below to see positive or negative result.
Step 4: Result

The emulsion test works because lipids are soluble in ethanol but insoluble in water. When the ethanol solution is poured into water, tiny lipid droplets form a cloudy white emulsion.
