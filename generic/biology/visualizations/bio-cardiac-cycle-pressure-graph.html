Hover over the graph to see pressure values and valve states at each point in time.
Key concept: Valves open and close in response to pressure differences. The AV valve closes when ventricular pressure exceeds atrial pressure (producing S1). The aortic valve closes when aortic pressure exceeds ventricular pressure (producing S2, with the dicrotic notch).
