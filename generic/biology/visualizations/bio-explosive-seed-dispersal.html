Explosive Seed Dispersal

How pods use tension from uneven drying to fling seeds away from the parent plant
Stage 1: Maturing Pod
MechanismUneven drying creates tension in pod walls. The outer layer dries and shrinks faster than the inner layer.
ReleaseSudden splitting along seams releases stored energy, twisting pod walls rapidly.
AdvantageSeeds spread away from parent plant, reducing competition for light, water and minerals.
