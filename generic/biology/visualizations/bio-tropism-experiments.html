Tropism Experiments

How plants respond to light and gravity through unequal distribution of auxin
1. Positive Phototropism
Auxin moves to shaded side → more growth there → shoot bends toward light
2. Gravitropism
Root: high auxin inhibits growth → bends down. Shoot: high auxin promotes growth → bends up
3. Tip Removed
No bending — the tip is where auxin is produced
4. Opaque Cap on Tip
No bending — light cannot reach tip to redistribute auxin
Auxin is a plant hormone produced in the shoot tip. It causes cell elongation. Light causes auxin to move to the shaded side.
