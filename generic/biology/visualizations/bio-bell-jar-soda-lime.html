Bell Jar Experiment: CO₂ and Photosynthesis

This experiment tests whether carbon dioxide is needed for photosynthesis. Hover over parts of the apparatus to learn their purpose.

Experimental
No CO₂ available
Control
CO₂ available
Results (after testing leaves with iodine)
Experimental (no CO₂):
Leaf stays brown/yellow with iodine.
No starch produced. Photosynthesis did not occur.
Control (CO₂ present):
Leaf turns blue-black with iodine.
Starch present. Photosynthesis occurred normally.

Conclusion: Carbon dioxide is required for photosynthesis. Without CO₂, the plant cannot produce starch.
