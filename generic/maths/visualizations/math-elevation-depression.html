Why Are They Equal?
The horizontal lines at the observer and the top of the building are parallel. The line of sight acts as a transversal. The angles of elevation and depression are alternate angles, so they are equal.
Line-of-Sight Distance
The angle of elevation looks up from horizontal. The angle of depression looks down from horizontal. They are equal because they are alternate angles between parallel lines.
