Domestic Electricity

Live (Brown)
Carries current at 230V. Alternates between +230V and -230V.
Neutral (Blue)
Completes the circuit. At 0V (earth potential).
Earth (Green/Yellow)
Safety wire. Carries current to ground if fault occurs.
The UK mains supply is 230V AC at 50Hz. The three-pin plug has live, neutral, and earth wires for safe electricity delivery.
