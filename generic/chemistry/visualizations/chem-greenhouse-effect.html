Natural Greenhouse

Without any greenhouse effect, Earth would be about -18 °C. Natural levels of CO\u2082, H\u2082O and CH\u2084 warm Earth to ~15 °C, making life possible.

Enhanced Greenhouse

Burning fossil fuels adds extra CO\u2082. More heat is trapped, raising global temperatures. This leads to climate change, ice melting and rising sea levels.
