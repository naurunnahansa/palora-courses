Fission: A heavy nucleus (e.g. U-235) absorbs a neutron and splits into two smaller daughter nuclei, releasing 2-3 neutrons and approximately 200 MeV of energy per fission.

Controlled: In a nuclear reactor, control rods absorb excess neutrons so that on average only 1 neutron per fission causes another fission (critical condition). Uncontrolled: Each fission triggers multiple further fissions, causing an exponential chain reaction.
